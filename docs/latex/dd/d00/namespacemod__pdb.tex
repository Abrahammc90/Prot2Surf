Allocate and initialize a {\ttfamily \doxylink{structmod__pdb_1_1type__pdb__file}{type\+\_\+pdb\+\_\+file}} structure from a PDB file.

Parse PDB records and populate the {\ttfamily \doxylink{structmod__pdb_1_1type__pdb__file}{type\+\_\+pdb\+\_\+file}} structure.

Reads ATOM/\+HETATM records from the already-\/open Fortran unit {\ttfamily pdb\+\_\+file}, fills {\ttfamily thisatoms} with atom records and populates {\ttfamily thisresidues} and {\ttfamily thischains} so that residues contain their per-\/residue {\ttfamily atoms} arrays and chains contain their residues and atoms. The routine expects {\ttfamily this} to have been allocated with the correct sizes (see {\ttfamily allocate\+\_\+pdb\+\_\+object}).
